# Accessibility

**Target:** WCAG 2.2 AA on iOS, Android and the portal, plus a 7:1 High contrast theme. **Legal floor:** WCAG 2.1 AA under the ADA Title II rule (April 26, 2027 for entities serving 50,000+ people) and the HHS Section 504 rule (May 11, 2027 for recipients with 15+ employees). When a contract says "ADA compliant", this is what it means (plan, module 15).

## Known gaps

- **The one exception: the day cells of the date calendar**, a note's and, since the phase 4
  review, a referral's (Add referral and Needs attention's Change the date, finding PX-3) and a
  problem's onset date (Add problem, finding C-3) (`docs/design/phase-4/forms.md` 8.2). The calendar is Material's, seven day columns to a row,
  and seven columns of 48 px need 336 px, more than a 320 px wide phone has: its cells are 47.7 px
  wide on a 390 px phone and 37.7 px on a 320 px one (WCAG 2.2's minimum is 24 px). They fail
  Android's 48 px check in every run and iOS's 44 px at 320 px. The way that meets every rule is
  the typed date beside the calendar: three private boxes, month, day and year, which pass every
  guideline in every run (three themes, 200% text, 320 px, and 200% text at 320 px), and the
  calendar stays as a second way. `apps/mobile/test/features/forms/date_field_test.dart` opens the
  calendar in every run with the guidelines on, holds its labels and contrast, and records which
  runs its cells can't meet, so a change to Material that fixes them shows;
  `apps/mobile/test/features/encounters/referrals_sheet_test.dart` does the same in the app, where
  the demo banner leaves the dialog less height and iOS's 44 px fails at 200% text too (cells 34.6
  px tall). Every date the phone takes is typed this way, phase 3's Add problem's onset date
  included since the review's second pass, and none of their calendars offers its own typing
  mode, whose box the keyboard would learn from.
- **A native select narrower than its chosen option's words cuts them at 320 px** (phase 5's
  verification). On the Team members pages a credential's or a custom role's select takes its
  column's width, as the page reflows; at 320 px, and sooner with text spacing, an option longer
  than that is cut in the closed select, as a native select draws it. Its picker shows every option
  whole, and a screen reader reads the value whole. The other way, the select at its own width, is
  the page scrolling sideways, which was the failure fixed. A later phase may replace these selects
  with radio groups.
- **The phone's focus look is faint.** It measures 1.16:1 to 1.63:1 against the surface in the
  three themes (phase 5's verification, VX4), where WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 of a focus indicator that
  is the only sign of focus. A focus ring for the whole app is its own unit, before phase 11's
  accessibility pass (`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`, risk 15).
- **Stacked tables split short words.** The portal's stacked table pattern breaks a cell's words
  anywhere, so a long value in one column can split short words in the others: the duplicates
  queue at 640 to 1024 px, and Notes not complete at 640 px with text spacing. The page never
  scrolls sideways. The Flags history's fix (cells wrap at spaces, free text anywhere), which the
  Phones page and a member's history took in phase 5, is the remedy, in phase 6's first unit
  (`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`, risk 14).

## What automated checks cannot prove

Automated tools find roughly a third of WCAG failures. Before each release and in phase 11:

- VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android) walk-throughs of every workflow.
- Keyboard-only walk-through of every portal workflow.
- Text at 200% on the phone and browser zoom at 200% on the portal, with no loss of content.
- An outside audit before the pilot, and an accessibility conformance report (VPAT 2.5, WCAG edition) as a sales document.
